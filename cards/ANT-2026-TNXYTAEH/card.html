ANT-2026-TNXYTAEH: Stack-buffer-overflow in libreoffice

The pptfuzzer harness was run against a single reproducer (/tmp/poc) and exited with a non-zero status (EXIT_CODE:1). No AddressSanitizer, UBSan, or other crash diagnostics were printed, so the crash type, faulting function, and access shape cannot be determined from this output alone. The target name suggests a PPT-format parsing entry point, but no source location or call chain is available.
