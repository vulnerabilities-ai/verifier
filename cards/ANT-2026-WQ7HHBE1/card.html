ANT-2026-WQ7HHBE1: Internal admin roles omitted from supautils reserved lists

The supautils config template lists roles the customer-facing postgres user is forbidden from touching, but leaves out supabase_functions_admin (owner of the supabase_functions schema) and supabase_privileged_role (the role supautils itself treats as privileged). On PostgreSQL 15 and earlier, CREATEROLE lets postgres ALTER/DROP any non-superuser role and GRANT it to anyone, so a customer can drop or password-hijack supabase_functions_admin or hand supabase_privileged_role to arbitrary app roles like authenticated. This enables self-DoS of platform webhooks, impersonation of the functions admin, and propagation of privileged-extension/privileged-GUC powers to end users. On PG16+ the new CREATEROLE-admin restriction largely mitigates it, and impact is confined to the customer's own project.

Target

Project: supabase/postgres
Location: ansible/files/postgresql_config/supautils.conf.j2:14
Discovery: static analysis — not yet dynamically reproduced

Technical Details

The reserved_roles (line 15) and reserved_memberships (line 14) allow-lists are incomplete: supabase_functions_admin and supabase_privileged_role are platform-internal but not reserved, so supautils does not block ALTER/DROP/GRANT on them. Because the customer postgres role has CREATEROLE, on PG ≤15 it can freely manipulate any non-superuser role that supautils fails to reserve.

Reproduction

  1. Connect to the project database as the customer postgres role (has CREATEROLE).
  2. Run ALTER ROLE supabase_functions_admin WITH PASSWORD '...' or DROP ROLE supabase_functions_admin.
  3. Optionally authenticate as supabase_functions_admin to use its net/http grants.
  4. Run GRANT supabase_privileged_role TO authenticated (or any app role) to propagate privileged powers.

[No reproducer or sanitizer output attached — request from security-cvd@anthropic.com if needed.]

Suggested Fix

Add supabase_functions_admin and supabase_privileged_role to both supautils.reserved_roles and supautils.reserved_memberships so customers cannot alter, drop, or grant membership in any platform-internal role.
