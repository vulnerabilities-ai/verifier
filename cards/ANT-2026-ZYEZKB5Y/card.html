ANT-2026-ZYEZKB5Y: Stack buffer overflow in pkcs15-crypt sign() via ASN1 sequence conversion without bounds

The sign() function in pkcs15-crypt performs an ASN.1 sequence conversion without bounds checking, overflowing a stack buffer.
