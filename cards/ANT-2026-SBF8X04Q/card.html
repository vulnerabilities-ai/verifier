ANT-2026-SBF8X04Q: Type confusion in CIL sensitivity alias resolution causing heap buffer overflow via alias struct cast to sensitivity struct

An alias struct is incorrectly cast to a sensitivity struct during CIL sensitivity alias resolution, producing a type-confused pointer and resulting in a heap buffer overflow.
