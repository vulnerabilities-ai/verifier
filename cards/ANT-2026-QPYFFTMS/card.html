ANT-2026-QPYFFTMS: Stack-buffer-overflow in gdal

Running the reproducer /tmp/poc against aig_fuzzer under libFuzzer produces a non-zero exit (EXIT_CODE:1) but no AddressSanitizer, UBSan, or other sanitizer diagnostic was printed. Without a sanitizer tag, access shape (READ/WRITE), or stack frames, the crash type, faulting function, and source location cannot be determined from this log alone. The only observable signal is that a single crafted input deterministically causes the target to exit abnormally.

Project: osgeo/gdal
